The challenge

Selling a machine in Russia is only half the job

Russian buyers almost always ask the same questions before they sign: who will install the machine, who will come if it stops, and how fast. Without a local answer, deals stall or go to a competitor with service on the ground.

  • remote support cannot replace an engineer next to the machine;
  • sending your own technicians abroad is slow and expensive;
  • time zones, language and documentation slow down every start-up;
  • every day of downtime at a customer's plant hurts your brand.

Building your own service team in Russia takes years to pay off. A local service partner solves it from the very first project.
